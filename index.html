About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Beihang University, advised by Prof. Zheng Zheng. My research focuses on reliable software engineering and AI agents for software development.

I study how program structure and repository evidence can support software comprehension, automated repair, and testing. My interests also include program analysis, specification discovery, and the validation of software changes.

I have worked with Huawei on trustworthy software and the reliability of AI systems, and with Wuhan Guide Infrared on infrared object detection and practical deployment.

02

Research Interests

My research connects software understanding with the evidence needed to assess the reliability of software systems and their changes.
  1. i.
    Reliable software engineering & AI agents

    AI agents for software development, automated program repair, and software testing.

  2. ii.
    Repository-level software comprehension

    Function-call graphs, structural representations, and retrieval-augmented generation for understanding software.

  3. iii.
    Program analysis & change validation

    Specification discovery and analysis of the properties that software changes should preserve.
