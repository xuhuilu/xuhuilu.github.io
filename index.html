ABOUT ME

I am Xuhui Lu, a Ph.D. candidate in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems at Beihang University, advised by Prof. Zheng Zheng. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research lies at the intersection of software engineering, program analysis, and artificial intelligence. My work spans structural analysis of software systems, repository-level program comprehension, and AI-assisted software engineering.

I am particularly interested in enabling software agents to understand complex repositories, reason about changes, and support trustworthy decisions through verifiable evidence.
